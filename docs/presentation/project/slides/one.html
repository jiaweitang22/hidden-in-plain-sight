How it works · one painting

Scatter every pixel along its line of sight

1

For each pixel of the painting, place one tiny coloured Gaussian somewhere along that pixel’s ray from the secret camera — at a random depth.

2

From the secret camera every speck lands on its own pixel. From anywhere else, parallax scatters them.

3

A short fit in gsplat cleans up overlaps; sizes stay between 0.4 and 1.5 pixels so the specks stay specks.

(0:35–0:55) The core idea is one sentence: put each pixel’s colour somewhere on its line of sight, at a random depth. From the secret camera they line up exactly; anywhere else, parallax scrambles them. Our first version had a problem: from the side the cloud looked like the picture stretched into a tube — every speck keeps its image row as you walk around. That observation led to the next step.
