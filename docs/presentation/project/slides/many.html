How it works · several paintings

Carve where every camera sees its painting

One cloud, three spots (front, side, above). Top: each painting and what its spot sees — SSIM 0.96 · 0.95 · 0.95. Bottom: every angle in between.

(0:55–1:25) To hide two or three paintings in one cloud, we only keep specks in the intersection of the cameras’ cones of sight — space carving, or stereo triangulation run backwards: instead of finding a 3D point from two matching pixels, we choose points so both projections land where we want. Each speck also gets a colour that changes with viewing angle — spherical-harmonics degree 1 — so it can be blue toward one camera and gold toward another. Then gsplat fits all views jointly. Result: three paintings, each above 0.94 SSIM, and noise everywhere in between.
