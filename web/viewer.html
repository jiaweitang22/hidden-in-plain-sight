How to look

Every cloud hides a masterpiece.

Each scene is one cloud of coloured specks. From almost everywhere it looks like noise, but every painting in it has one secret viewpoint where the specks line up into the picture.

Circle the cloud and watch the warmer or colder ring. When you get close, the painting assembles out of the noise. Stuck? Drag to assemble, or press Show me a painting. Paintings stay a mystery here until you find them.
